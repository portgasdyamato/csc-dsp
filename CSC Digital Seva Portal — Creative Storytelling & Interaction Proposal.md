# **CSC Digital Seva Portal — Creative Storytelling & Interaction Proposal**

Creative experience direction · Landing page · Interaction design · Storytelling

## **1\. The vision: turn a service portal into a human story**

**Hero headline:** Bringing Essential Services Closer to Every Indian.

The goal is not just to redesign the CSC landing page or make it look better. It is to help visitors understand the purpose of CSC, recognise the people behind the network, discover the services available to them, and explore the opportunity of becoming a CSC VLE.

The site should feel like an unfolding story of India's digital services ecosystem. Instead of repeating a heading, a paragraph and a row of cards in every section, we use different storytelling formats:

* Cinematic photography and short documentary videos

* Scroll-led visual narratives

* Interactive maps and visual data storytelling

* Perspective changes and immersive transitions

* Interactive service discovery

* Real VLE stories and experiences

* Small, meaningful interactions that explain processes

**Central creative principle:** Don't just tell visitors what CSC does. Show them the people, experiences and possibilities behind it.

The storytelling can be emotional, but the interface must stay practical. A citizen should find a service quickly. A prospective VLE should understand the opportunity without watching a video or waiting for an animation.

## **2\. The overall narrative**

One continuous narrative, moving from a national network to an individual experience:

1. **Purpose** — what CSC brings closer to people.

2. **Scale** — how the network reaches across India.

3. **People** — the VLEs and citizens behind the ecosystem.

4. **Possibilities** — services and entrepreneurship opportunities.

5. **Emotional connection** — real stories and experiences.

6. **Confidence** — process, support and safety.

7. **Action** — find a CSC or become a VLE.

## **3\. Landing page structure and creative execution**

### **01\. Important notice — keep people informed**

**Format:** Notice strip \+ expandable announcement

A functional entry point, not a storytelling-heavy section. Critical announcements must be visible immediately.

* Compact announcement strip above the main navigation.

* Small visual indicator for important or urgent notices.

* Clicking opens the full announcement in a readable panel.

* Show publication date and audience: citizens, VLEs or both.

* Provide a direct action link where needed.

*Illustrative content: replace with an approved CSC notice.*

### **02\. Header — make the journeys immediately accessible**

**Format:** Minimal navigation with clear hierarchy

Navigation should make clear that the portal serves different audiences.

**Proposed items:** About CSC · Explore Services · Become a VLE · Find a CSC · News & Updates · Help & Support · Login · Language selector

**Interaction:** On scroll, the header becomes a compact sticky bar. On mobile, the two key actions, Find a CSC and Become a VLE, stay easy to reach.

### **03\. Hero — introduce the human story**

**Signature storytelling moment**

**Headline:** Bringing Essential Services Closer to Every Indian.

**Supporting line:** Helping citizens access government and business services through India's Common Service Centres and the people behind them.

**Primary CTA:** Become a VLE · **Secondary CTA:** Explore CSC Services

**Creative concept: a day, a need, a human interaction.**

Don't use a generic image of a computer, digital network or India map in the hero. Tell a small human story through a cinematic visual sequence. (The India map belongs in the impact section, not here.)

**Proposed 10–15 second video sequence:**

1. A citizen arrives at a CSC with a specific everyday need.

2. The VLE welcomes them and understands what they need.

3. The VLE assists them using the digital service platform.

4. The citizen receives guidance and leaves with clarity.

5. The scene widens to show the CSC environment.

The video should establish the value of a CSC through an actual human interaction.

**Visual treatment**

* Documentary-style footage, not stock-looking advertising.

* Natural Indian environments, real CSC equipment, authentic people.

* Warm, natural lighting.

* Cinematic transitions with restrained motion.

* Short, compressed video with a static-image fallback.

**Interaction:** The video plays silently and can be paused. The headline, description and CTAs are always visible. The hero must communicate its message without anyone watching the full video.

*Visual references: Best CSC Center Hinjewadi Pune (Shripada Enterprises); JAN AI – Supporting Rural India; E-Governance Solutions – Mantra Identity Solutions.*

### **04\. CSC impact — show the scale, don't just display numbers**

**Headline:** A Nationwide Network. Millions of Possibilities.

**Format:** Animated India map \+ progressive data reveal

Instead of four statistic boxes side by side, turn the scale of the network into a visual narrative.

**Interaction sequence**

1. **The map appears.** A clean outline of India gradually emerges.

2. **The first location appears.** One CSC lights up, introducing the idea of a local centre.

3. **The network grows.** More points appear across the map, revealing the nationwide network.

4. **The numbers become visible.** Verified statistics appear alongside the map as it expands.

5. **The visitor explores.** Optional state-level selection reveals verified regional information.

**Statistics to showcase**

* Total functional CSCs

* CSCs at Gram Panchayat level

* Geographic reach across India

* Number or categories of services

Numbers are not decorative counters. Each needs a clear label, source and reporting date. Earlier figures in our research are historical and must be updated before publication.

**Implementation notes**

* Dots should represent real geographic data where available. If accurate coordinates are unavailable, use an explicitly illustrative animation rather than presenting invented locations as real centres.

* Provide a static equivalent so the animation never delays or blocks access to the information.

*Visual references: abstract India network map; glowing India silhouette made of dots and lines.*

### **05\. Choose your journey — two sides of the same ecosystem**

**Headline:** Every Journey Begins with a Different Need.

**Format:** Alternating split-screen visual storytelling

Introduces the two primary audiences without making them feel like two unrelated websites.

#### **05A — For aspiring VLEs**

**Turn Your Ambition into an Opportunity.**  Explore what it means to become a Village Level Entrepreneur, understand the requirements and discover how to establish your own CSC enterprise.

* Understand the VLE role.

* Explore eligibility and requirements.

* Learn about the registration process.

* Discover available opportunities.

**CTA:** Explore What It Means to Be a CSC VLE

**Interaction: the VLE journey unfolds.** A simple horizontal or vertical indicator: Explore → Check eligibility → Register → Verification → Get started. Selecting a stage reveals a short explanation and what the applicant needs to prepare. This makes the process understandable before anyone enters a lengthy registration flow.

#### **05B — For citizens**

**The Help You Need. The Services You Can Access.** From everyday payments to essential government services, discover what may be available through your nearest Common Service Centre.

* Explore available services.

* Understand what documents may be required.

* Find a nearby CSC.

* Get assistance from a local VLE.

**CTA:** Find a CSC

**Interaction: a citizen's everyday needs.** A small visual sequence of common needs, such as paying a bill, applying for a certificate or accessing a banking service. Selecting a need leads to the relevant service information. Citizens should never have to understand CSC's internal service categories before finding what they need. With every scroll, the visual changes and the storytelling happens. The text on the other side will stay the same, but the visual will change with every scroll as it's like telling the story. 

**Layout rule:** Both split sections use matching proportions, image dimensions, typography and spacing. Keep all content within its own column. Stacked vertically like top and bottom in this section. Inside cards will be horizontal: image on the right side and the text on the left side, or vice versa logically on mobile.

### **06\. Services — turn discovery into an experience**

**Headline:** One Centre. A World of Essential Services.

**Format:** Interactive service explorer

**Interface concept**

* Two primary filters: G2C and B2C.

* Search field: "What are you looking for?"

* Search suggestions based on everyday needs.

* Horizontally scrollable service cards.

* "Explore all services" link.

**Creative interaction: start with a need, not a category.**

*What would you like help with? Choose a common need to explore relevant services.*

Certificates · Bill payments · Banking · Insurance

*Illustrative categories. Actual services and availability must be verified.*

Once a visitor picks a need, show relevant services, eligibility or document information where available, and the next step.

**Service cards contain:** service name, G2C or B2C label, short explanation, explore action, and verified prerequisites where available.

**Carousel rules:** it must never hijack normal page scrolling. Support touch, keyboard, visible arrows and a clear no-results state.

*Visual references: JAN AI – Supporting Rural India; Shopian's DIGI-Pay Sakhi delivering services in far-off villages (Greater Kashmir).*

### **07\. VLE opportunity — explain the actual opportunity**

**Headline:** Explore What It Means to Be a CSC VLE.

**Format:** Interactive feature storytelling \+ visual walkthrough

This section covers the VLE role, benefits, responsibilities and opportunities, and helps prospective entrepreneurs imagine what running a CSC involves.

**Creative concept: step inside the world of a CSC VLE.** Instead of six generic benefit cards, introduce an interactive CSC workspace. Visitors explore the different parts of operating a centre.

*Select an element to discover what it means to operate a CSC.*

| Element | What it opens |
| :---- | :---- |
| Digital services | Access and deliver eligible services |
| Your enterprise | Centre setup and operations |
| Training | Applicable training and certification |
| Earning model | Service commissions and business considerations |
| Support | Applicable guidance and support resources |
| Community | The VLE's role in serving citizens |

*Conceptual interaction. Each element opens a detailed explanation. It must not imply that every facility is automatically provided to every VLE.*

**Supporting content beneath the interaction**

| Topic | Information to explain |
| :---- | :---- |
| Role | What a VLE does and how a CSC operates |
| Services | Types of services a VLE may offer |
| Business | How service-based commissions and revenue work |
| Infrastructure | Computer, printer, internet and centre requirements |
| Training | TEC and other applicable certifications |
| Registration | Eligibility, documents, verification and onboarding |

**Additional interaction: a visual day in the life of a VLE.** A short scroll-led sequence: opening the centre → preparing the equipment → helping a citizen with a service request → completing a transaction → managing the centre and exploring additional services. This should be an illustrative or documented scenario, not a promise that every VLE performs the same tasks.

**CTA:** Explore VLE Eligibility & Registration

*Visual references: JAN AI – Supporting Rural India; Mantra Identity Solutions; Rajasthan e-Mitra network expansion (ETGovernment).*

### **08\. VLE success stories — make the people the proof**

**Headline:** Real People. Real Journeys. Real Experiences.

**Format:** Documentary storytelling \+ interactive story gallery

This is where the site becomes emotionally memorable. Instead of generic testimonials, feature actual VLEs and their experiences, so visitors understand the journey through people who have lived it.

**Creative execution: the people behind the network.** Each story opens with a full-width portrait or short video, followed by a first-person introduction.

*Example editorial format:* **Bringing digital India into tribal hinterlands of Odisha**

*VLE story · illustrative layout*

*"My CSC journey started with a simple idea."*

A first-person account of why the entrepreneur started, what they learned, the challenges they faced and how their centre serves people today.

*Names, locations and stories are added only with the VLE's permission and verified details.*

**Story formats to experiment with**

* **Portrait stories:** a photograph and a short personal narrative.

* **Mini documentaries:** 30–60 second videos following a VLE's working day.

* **Audio stories:** first-person accounts in the VLE's preferred language.

* **Journey timelines:** starting point, learning experience and milestones.

* **Regional stories:** different states, communities, languages and centre environments.

**Creative interaction: choose a story, discover a perspective.** Visitors can filter by state, experience or story theme. A story opens into a more immersive editorial view with photographs, video and quotes.

This section should show the diversity of the CSC network, not just a few polished success stories.

*Visual references: Wired for Work (digital infrastructure and job creation); Bringing digital India into tribal hinterlands of Odisha.*

### **09\. Conversion — turn interest into action**

**Headline:** Your CSC Journey Starts with One Step.

**Format:** High-impact visual \+ focused CTA

After visitors have learned about CSC, explored the opportunities and heard from VLEs, give them a clear next step.

**For prospective VLEs:** Explore the requirements, understand the registration process and take the first step towards becoming a Village Level Entrepreneur.

* **Primary CTA:** Start VLE Registration

* **Secondary CTA:** Check Eligibility

**For existing applicants:** Track Application · Resume Application

**Creative interaction: a simple readiness preview.** Before redirecting to registration, show a short preparation guide:

1. Check the minimum eligibility criteria.

2. Understand centre infrastructure requirements.

3. Prepare the required documents.

4. Review TEC and other applicable requirements.

5. Proceed to the official registration process.

This is a preparation aid, not another complicated form. Include a clear reminder to use official CSC channels and to beware of fraudulent registration or payment requests.

*Visual reference: From unstable income to cyber cafe: Ambedkar Nagar's digital hub (YourStory).*

### **10\. News and updates — keep the network alive**

**Headline:** What's Happening Across the CSC Network?

**Format:** Editorial timeline \+ announcement cards

The network is continually evolving. This section communicates what is changing and what visitors need to know.

**Content categories:** new services and initiatives · VLE opportunities and training · government announcements · events and programmes · operational updates · important deadlines.

**Creative execution: an evolving network timeline.** Use a clean editorial timeline rather than an endless stack of promotional banners. Every update answers three questions:

1. What has changed?

2. Who does it affect?

3. What should the visitor do next?

Give important announcements higher visual priority, and keep older news accessible without letting outdated information dominate the page.

### **11\. Trust, safety and support — make the experience feel reliable**

**Headline:** Your Safety Is Part of Every Service.

**Format:** Interactive awareness \+ accessible support hub

Digital services are only useful when people understand how to access them safely. This section helps citizens and VLEs recognise official channels, identify fraudulent communication and know where to get help.

**Support experience**

* Helpdesk and contact information

* Frequently asked questions

* Grievance redressal

* Official communication channels

* Registration and application assistance

* Security and fraud awareness

The most important support actions should also appear contextually near registration, login and payment information, not only at the bottom of the page.

### **12\. Footer — continue the relationship**

**Headline:** Explore More. Stay Informed. Stay Connected.

The footer is a clear directory for the whole CSC ecosystem.

| Group | Links |
| :---- | :---- |
| About CSC | About, mission, network |
| For VLEs | Eligibility, registration, resources |
| For citizens | Services, find a CSC |
| Support | Helpdesk, FAQs, grievance |
| Updates | News, announcements |
| Legal | Privacy, terms, policies |
| Connect | Official communication channels |

Use official CSC branding, verified contact details and relevant legal links.

## **4\. How the storytelling techniques work together**

We should not use the same interaction format for every section. Each technique needs a reason to exist.

| Technique | Where | Purpose |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Cinematic video storytelling | Hero, VLE stories | Real people, environments and moments create emotional connection |
| Data-led visual storytelling | Impact | Turn network statistics into a visual explanation of national reach |
| Interactive discovery | Services | Let people explore by need instead of reading a catalogue |
| Explorable environments | VLE opportunity | Make the role, tools and responsibilities understandable through an interactive CSC workspace |
| Editorial and documentary storytelling | VLE stories | Real people communicate the value of the network in their own words |
| Guided interactions | Registration, eligibility, safety | Reduce uncertainty and show what to do next |

## **5\. Signature experience ideas**

Beyond the individual sections, these interactions could make the landing page distinctive.

### **A. The same CSC, different perspectives**

Use one recurring CSC environment throughout the page.

* First, visitors see the centre from the outside.

* In the VLE section, they experience it from behind the counter.

* In the citizen section, they see it from the visitor's side.

* In the impact section, the single centre becomes part of the wider network.

This connects the VLE and citizen journeys without forcing them into identical experiences.

### **B. A day at a CSC**

A short scroll-led documentary sequence showing the centre through a typical day.

| Moment | Visual |
| :---- | :---- |
| Opening | The VLE prepares the centre |
| First visitor | A citizen arrives with a service need |
| Assistance | The VLE helps with a digital service |
| Everyday operations | Different service interactions |
| Closing | The centre wraps up its day |

This could be a mid-page storytelling experience or a separate feature on the VLE journey.

### **C. Explore the CSC network**

An interactive map that lets visitors move from India to a state, district and available centre information. It could eventually support the Find a CSC journey, but the locator itself must work without interacting with the animation.

### **D. Build your CSC journey**

An interactive explainer where prospective VLEs discover:

* What infrastructure is required.

* Which qualifications and certifications may apply.

* What documents are needed.

* How registration and verification work.

* What happens after approval.

This is more useful than a decorative 3D model of a computer kiosk.

## **6\. Visual direction and design language**

**A contemporary Indian digital identity, told through people.**

Combine the credibility of an official national platform with the warmth of human documentary storytelling.

* **Photography:** realistic, diverse, people-first documentary images.

* **Video:** short, purposeful scenes rather than long promotional films.

* **Interface:** clean layouts, strong hierarchy, familiar interactions.

* **Motion:** subtle geographic reveals, transitions and progressive data visualisation.

**Design language**

* Retain CSC's established orange and navy identity, using its official design tokens.

* Use a readable, modern type system with strong support for Indian scripts.

* Use authentic Indian environments, not stereotypical or overly staged rural imagery.

* Mix full-width cinematic scenes with clean functional sections.

* Give photographs and videos room to tell stories.

* Use motion to explain relationships, journeys and data, not to decorate.

## **8\. What success should look like**

The creative experience must improve real user outcomes, not just look impressive.

| Goal | What to measure |
| :---- | :---- |
| Awareness | Hero engagement, audience journey selection, scroll depth |
| Understanding | VLE information engagement, eligibility visits, process comprehension |
| Service discovery | Search usage, filter usage, service detail engagement |
| Conversion | Registration starts, completions, drop-off and resume rate |
| Citizen access | Find-a-CSC usage and successful locator journeys |
| Trust | Support engagement, scam-awareness comprehension |
| Retention | Returning visitors, update engagement, repeat VLE resource visits |

Use a baseline from the existing portal and compare after launch. Run usability testing with prospective VLEs, existing VLEs and citizens from different regions. Test animations and storytelling for comprehension, not just visual appeal.

## **9\. Implementation priorities**

Not every creative idea needs to be built at once.

**Phase 1 — Core experience (highest priority)**

* Human-centred hero video or image

* Clear VLE and citizen journeys

* Interactive service discovery

* VLE opportunity and registration information

* Find-a-CSC and support access

**Phase 2 — Signature storytelling (differentiation)**

* Animated India network map

* Same CSC, different perspectives

* Documentary VLE stories

* Interactive VLE workspace

**Phase 3 — Rich experiences (enhancement)**

* Scroll-led day-at-a-CSC sequence

* Interactive network explorer

* Regional video and audio stories

* More advanced service discovery and guided explainers

## **10\. Final creative proposal**

**Bringing Essential Services Closer to Every Indian.** *A human story behind a nationwide network of digital services.*

The redesigned CSC landing page moves beyond a conventional, information-heavy portal and becomes an engaging, human-centred digital experience. Through documentary visuals, interactive storytelling, data-led animation and guided discovery, visitors will understand the CSC ecosystem, explore services, discover the VLE opportunity and build confidence in taking their next step.

**The creative ambition:** Make the network visible. Make the services understandable. Make the people memorable. Make the next step effortless.

The goal isn't to make every section an animation. It's to choose the right storytelling medium for each message, and make the whole journey feel like one coherent story.
